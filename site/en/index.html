Your local media center, console-style.

Videos, music and photos across your PC, phone and TV, right on your home network. No USB sticks, no server, no cloud.

View on GitHub Roadmap Community

One tap. Another screen.

Console UI

Fullscreen and fully usable with a TV remote, gamepad or arrow keys. Built for the couch.

Video, music and photos

Subtitles, audio tracks, music queue, slideshows and resume where you left off.

No server

Every device is a node. They find each other on your home network (mDNS) and share media directly.

Play on another screen

From your phone or PC, send playback to the TV and control play, pause and seek.

Secure and private

Login on every device, PIN pairing and encrypted connections. Nothing leaves your home.

Truly free

GPL-3.0 open source. Optional telemetry, off by default, with a public event catalog.

Runs on

Linux (Manjaro, Arch, Ubuntu, Fedora) Android Android TV Samsung TV (Tizen) Later: Windows, macOS, iOS
